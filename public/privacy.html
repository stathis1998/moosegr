Privacy Policy

Moose Software Solutions EE is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide when you submit a request through the contact form on our website.

What information do we collect?

When you use our contact form, we collect the following personal data: First and Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and any personal information you voluntarily include in the message field.

How do we use your information?

We collect this data solely for the purpose of reviewing your project inquiry or question, and contacting you to provide the requested information, a proposal, or support. Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the legal bases we rely on are your explicit consent (provided when submitting the form) and the necessity of taking pre-contractual steps at your request.

Do we share your information?

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may share your data with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website and business, such as our form delivery service (Web3Forms) and email service provider (Google Workspace). These providers are legally obligated to protect your data and only process it according to our strict instructions.

How long do we keep your information?

We will only retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes we collected it for. Inquiries that do not result in an active business relationship will be securely deleted after 6 months. If you become a client, your data will be retained for the duration of our business relationship and as required by Greek tax and commercial law.

How do we keep your information safe?

Moose Software Solutions EE acts as the primary data controller. We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to ensure your personal data is protected against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse throughout its lifecycle.

What are your privacy rights?

Under GDPR, you have the right to request copies of your personal data, request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete information, or request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions. You can also withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

How can you contact us about this policy?

If you have any questions or wish to exercise your data protection rights, you can reach us through the following methods:

  1. By email at info@moose.gr.
  2. By mail at our registered business address at Leoforos Kifisias 44, Marousi 151 25, Athens, Greece.
  3. We have one month to respond to any formal requests regarding your data rights.
